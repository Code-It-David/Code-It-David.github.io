Gold Break Rush

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 August 2026

This policy explains how Kami Light Studio (“we”) handles information when you use the Gold Break Rush Android game.

Information handled by the game

The game does not require an account and we do not ask you to enter your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or files. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may remove this local data.

Advertising

Gold Break Rush uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertising. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform asks for your privacy choices before ads are requested. A “Privacy options” control is available in the game whenever the platform requires it.

Purchases

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The game checks Google Play for the purchase state and stores only a local entitlement needed to remember whether advertising should be disabled.

Third-party services

Children

The game is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The age group and content rating shown on Google Play are the authoritative audience settings for the release.

Data retention and choices

Local gameplay and entitlement data remains on your device until the app data is cleared or the app is removed. Purchase records are retained by Google under its own policies. You may manage advertising and consent choices through the in-game privacy control when available and through your Android and Google account settings.

Security and international processing

Third-party SDK data is transmitted using encrypted connections. Google may process information in countries other than your own under its published privacy safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a material revision is published.
